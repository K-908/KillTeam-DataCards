# Checkpoint 3: Lay out both sides of the sheets

## Goal

Convert the ordered card list into a selected A4 or US Letter layout for double-sided printing.

## Tasks

- Default to A4 portrait (210 x 297 mm) with four 121 x 70 mm card slots in one centered column and 2 mm gaps.
- For US Letter portrait (215.9 x 279.4 mm), use three vertically stacked cards and 6 mm gaps. Four full-size cards cannot fit vertically.
- For the 2 x 2 option, use A4 or US Letter portrait with four cards rotated 90 degrees into 70 x 121 mm slots and 6 mm gaps.
- Group cards by the selected layout's capacity, keeping each front/reverse sheet pair together.
- Keep reverse slots in the same top-to-bottom order for the vertical layout. Swap left/right columns on 2 x 2 reverse sheets. Use portrait long-edge duplex for both layouts. Do not mirror the artwork itself.
- Leave matching reverse slots blank where the last front sheet has unused slots.
- Add subtle cutting guides outside or at card edges without obscuring artwork.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eleven cards produce three front/reverse sheet pairs for A4 vertical or either 2 x 2 layout, and four pairs for Letter vertical.
- Front and reverse positions match for every slot, including a mix of paired and shared reverses.
- Card artwork is not stretched or clipped; differing source aspect ratios are fitted within the slot.
- The selected gap remains clear and guides do not cover card content.

Checkpoint 2.
